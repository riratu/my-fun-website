How to book a bike ticket from Switzerland to Italy

An easy-to-follow guide. Estimated time: 2 evenings.
1
Search for a connection with a bike
Go to sbb.ch. Enter your route. Find some nice fast connections.
continue
2
Find the bike filter
The bike option is not on the page where you book tickets. Look around. Find it under "more options" after some searching.
continue
3
Find great connections
The connections look even better now. Much more options. Select one, choose a seat, proceed through the entire booking flow. At the very end: "No bike places available." The bike filter goes missing when you change the time. if you forget to add a bike ticket at the end, you have to refund the ticket and get a new one. you can't just buy a bike ticket.
continue
4
Re-add the bike filter and try again
Go back. Re-add the bike. Find a connection. Proceed to payment. Enter your Halbtax. It asks for a subscription number. The number printed on your Halbtax (Half Fare Card) does not fit into the field. In a forum post you find that you have to enter SWISSPA followed by the number. But not for trains that are completely in Switzerland. Only for the international part. For domestic Swiss trains you can't enter the Halbtax at all.
continue
5
Go to the ticket office
You give up on the website. Go to the SBB ticket office in person. The person at the counter tells you they cannot book bike tickets for trains in Italy. They suggest you try the Trenitalia website.
continue
6
Find a better connection on Trenitalia
On the Trenitalia website you find a better connection anyway. But you can't book it there. Turns out you need another website for this specific part of the journey. You need to use the Trenord website.
continue
7
Create an account on Trenord
Register on Trenord. You have to enter your country of residence and place of birth. The dropdown only lists Italian cities. There is no option for another country.
continue
8
Ask a friend from Italy
You write a friend who lives in Italy. He says you need to type ESTERO in the field to select a foreign country. This is not documented anywhere.
continue
9
Calculate your Codice Fiscale
Trenord requires your Italian fiscal code (Codice Fiscale). You are not Italian. You don't have one. But you must enter one. It is derived from your name, birth date, gender, and place of birth. Ask an AI to generate it for you. Tell it to use code to verify the check character (Prüfziffer), because otherwise it will be wrong.
continue
10
Enter your address
You can't choose an address in a foreign country, you have to choose a city in Italy. Just entering an Italian city is probably illegal, but would hypothetically work.
continue
11
Download the Trenord app
If you book via the website, they will ship you a physical card by mail. To Italy. Where you don't live. Download the Trenord app instead.
continue
12
Try to pay
Select "pay with credit card." The payment is declined. You are sent back to the homepage. All your data is gone. Enter everything again.
continue
13
Try to pay again (x4)
Try credit card again. Declined. Try PayPal. Declined. Each time you are sent back to the homepage.
Tip: Only select a bike, not the travelers. This way you don't have to re-enter the birthdate for each person every time through the cumbersome interface.
continue
14
Payment works
One of the attempts finally goes through. Now do it again, this time with both travelers and the additional bike.
continue
15
Realise you selected the wrong date
You purchased tickets for today instead of your travel date. The tickets are non-refundable and the date cannot be changed.
continue
16
Purchase the tickets again
Buy the same tickets again, for the correct date this time.
continue
That's it. It is this simple.
